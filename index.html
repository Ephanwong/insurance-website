What I Offer

Insurance Solutions for Every Life Stage

Whether you're protecting your home, health, car, or travel plans, I'll help you find the right Tokio Marine coverage at the right value.

🏠
Property Insurance

Protect your home or business premises against fire, flood, theft, and unexpected structural damage. Your property is likely your biggest asset — guard it properly.

  • Fire & perils coverage
  • Flood & natural disaster protection
  • Theft & burglary coverage
  • Renovation & content protection
Ideal for: Homeowners, landlords, SME business owners
🚗
Motor Insurance

Comprehensive or third-party motor coverage that protects you from accident costs, theft, and liability — so you drive with complete peace of mind.

  • Comprehensive & third-party options
  • Windshield & flood add-ons
  • 24/7 roadside assistance
  • Smooth, fast claims processing
Ideal for: Car owners, families with multiple vehicles
🏥
Medical Insurance

Rising healthcare costs shouldn't stop you from accessing the best treatment. Our medical plans cover hospitalisation, specialist care, and critical illness treatments.

  • Hospitalisation & surgery coverage
  • Specialist & outpatient care
  • Critical illness riders available
  • Annual & lifetime limit options
Ideal for: Individuals, families, employees & retirees
🦺
Personal Accident Insurance

Accidents happen without warning. This coverage provides financial support for you and your family in the event of accidental injury, disability, or death.

  • Accidental death & permanent disability
  • Medical expense reimbursement
  • 24-hour worldwide coverage
  • Affordable annual premiums
Ideal for: Working adults, parents, manual workers
✈️
Travel Insurance

Travel confidently knowing you're covered for trip cancellations, medical emergencies abroad, lost luggage, and flight delays — wherever your journey takes you.

  • Emergency medical overseas
  • Trip cancellation & curtailment
  • Lost baggage & personal belongings
  • Individual, family & group plans
Ideal for: Frequent travelers, families on holiday

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about insurance? Here are the most common ones I hear — answered in plain language.

Medical emergencies are unpredictable. A single hospitalisation in a private hospital can cost RM 20,000–RM 100,000 or more. Medical insurance protects your savings and ensures you receive treatment without financial stress. The younger and healthier you are when you start, the lower your premium.
When an insured event occurs, you notify me immediately. I guide you through the required documentation, submit the claim to Tokio Marine on your behalf, and follow up until settlement. My role is to make the process as smooth and stress-free as possible for you — you never have to navigate it alone.
For families, I typically recommend prioritising: (1) Medical insurance for all members, (2) Personal accident coverage, (3) Property insurance for your home, and (4) Motor insurance. The right combination depends on your family size, age, income, and lifestyle — which is exactly what I help you assess during a free consultation.
Absolutely. Tokio Marine offers flexible plans with various riders and add-ons. For example, you can add flood protection to your property policy, or include critical illness coverage on top of your medical plan. During our consultation, I'll help you build a personalised package that fits your needs and budget precisely.
Employer-provided coverage is often limited — it may not cover your dependants, typically ceases when you leave the company, and usually has low benefit limits. Personal insurance fills these gaps, continues regardless of employment, and can be tailored to your specific family needs. I can review your existing employer coverage and recommend what supplementary protection makes sense.
At minimum, once a year — which is exactly the service I provide to all my clients.
